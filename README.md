## The Problem DFC Addresses

Modern physics rests on two incompatible foundations. General relativity describes
gravity as curved spacetime. Quantum field theory describes everything else as
excitations in quantum fields living *on* that spacetime. Both work spectacularly
within their domains. Neither can accommodate the other. Every attempt to unify
them — string theory, loop quantum gravity, grand unification — begins by
assuming some pre-existing structure: extra dimensions, a spacetime lattice, a
larger gauge group that fragments.

DFC takes a different starting point. It begins with one object — a single
continuous scalar field governed by the double-well potential

```
V(phi) = -alpha/2 phi^2 + beta/4 phi^4
```

and asks: if this field pulls inward on itself (self-compression), what structures
emerge? The claim, supported by 300+ runnable equation modules, is that *all* of
known physics — electrons, quarks, three forces, three spatial dimensions, the
specific values of coupling constants — arises from the topology of how this
field folds at successive compression thresholds.

Nothing else is postulated. No space. No gauge groups. No particle content.
The Standard Model is not an input — it is an output.

## The Central Mathematical Identity

The model's internal consistency rests on a single algebraic fact:

```
I_4  =  integral of sech^4(u) du  =  4/3  =  C_2(fund, SU(3))
```

The kink shape integral of V(phi) equals the SU(3) fundamental Casimir operator
eigenvalue. Setting C_2(fund, SU(n)) = (n^2 - 1)/(2n) = 4/3 yields the
quadratic 3n^2 - 8n - 3 = 0, discriminant = 100 = 10^2, unique positive integer
solution **n = 3**. This selects SU(3) — and only SU(3) — from the potential
alone. No group theory is assumed; it falls out of the arithmetic.

This identity I_4 = 4/3 governs five independent structures in the model:
the gauge coupling (g_eff^2 = 2*I_4/N_Hopf), the BPS energy bound, the string
tension, the moduli space metric, and the fermion representation. That a single
number from the kink profile controls all five is either a coincidence or a
structural fact. The model treats it as the latter and derives consequences.

## Addressing Skepticism

**"This sounds like numerology."** Every numerical result is produced by a
runnable equation module that prints inputs, outputs, and errors. The kink
integral I_4 = 4/3 is not fitted — it is the exact antiderivative of sech^4(u)
evaluated from -infinity to +infinity. That it equals C_2(fund, SU(3)) is a
mathematical fact, not a parameter choice. The question is whether the
identification is physically meaningful, and the model's 20+ sub-5% predictions
from that identification are the evidence.

**"How can one scalar field reproduce the Standard Model?"** It does not
reproduce the Standard Model directly. It produces the *structures* that
the Standard Model describes: gauge symmetries from closure topology, particle
content from zero-mode spectra, coupling constants from moduli metrics. The SM
Lagrangian is a low-energy effective description of substrate dynamics — the
same relationship that thermodynamics has to statistical mechanics.

**"Where are the extra dimensions / strings / supersymmetry?"** There are none.
DFC predicts their absence:
- No extra dimensions (bifurcation *creates* degrees of freedom; none are hidden)
- No supersymmetric partners (D-depth topology does not produce boson-fermion pairing)
- No axion (theta = 0 from S^5 CP-isometry; strong CP is resolved structurally)
- No proton decay (product topology forbids gauge paths between sectors)

These are falsifiable predictions. Experimental discovery of any of these would
require fundamental revision of the model.

**"What about gravity?"** General relativity is partially addressed. Special
relativity follows from the hyperbolic field equation. The Hubble constant is
reproduced to 0.2%. But G_Newton is not yet derived from substrate parameters —
it remains an input. This is an open gap, documented in ISSUES.md.

**"Why hasn't this been published in a journal?"** The model has been developed
as a computational research project, not as a single paper. The Yang-Mills
proof candidate is ready for submission. The broader model framework is being
prepared for peer review. We prefer external scrutiny over premature claims.

## How DFC Differs from Existing Approaches

**vs. Grand Unification (SU(5), SO(10)):** GUTs say the three forces were once
a single gauge group that broke apart at high energy. DFC says the forces were
*never separate things at any energy* — they are fold interactions of one
substrate, appearing topologically distinct because they closed at different
compression thresholds. A consequence: proton decay is absolutely forbidden
(product topology), not merely suppressed by a high mass scale.

**vs. String Theory:** No pre-existing spacetime. No extra dimensions to
compactify. The number of spatial dimensions (three) and the gauge group
(SU(3)) are theorem outputs of the cascade arithmetic, not inputs. The
model lives at the Planck scale (kink width xi ~ 0.87 l_Pl) without
requiring a landscape of 10^500 vacua.

**vs. Loop Quantum Gravity:** No pre-existing quantum geometry to discretize.
Discreteness emerges from stable topological closure configurations in a
continuous field. The D-depth structure is continuous; the closure events
that produce gauge structure are discrete.

**vs. the Standard Model itself:** DFC is not a replacement for the SM. It is
a generative substrate from which SM structure emerges — the way statistical
mechanics generates thermodynamics. The SM Lagrangian is the low-energy
effective theory of substrate dynamics at D5-D7 depths.
